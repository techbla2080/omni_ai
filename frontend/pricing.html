Common questions.

Can I cancel anytime? +
Yes. Cancel from your settings and you'll keep Pro access until the end of your current billing period. No fees, no questions.
What payment methods do you accept? +
UPI, credit cards, debit cards, and net banking — all through Razorpay. Business users get auto-generated GST invoices.
What happens if I hit my message limit? +
On Free, you'll wait until tomorrow (limits reset daily at midnight IST). On Pro, your monthly limit resets on your billing date. If you regularly hit the cap, talk to us — we'll work something out.
Is my data private? +
Yes. We never train models on your conversations. Your data is encrypted at rest. Delete anything, anytime, from your settings.
Can I switch from monthly to annual later? +
Yes. Upgrade to annual anytime from your billing settings — you'll get a prorated credit for what you've already paid.
Do you offer refunds? +
If Pro isn't right for you within the first 7 days, we'll refund you in full. Just email hello@omniai.biz.
